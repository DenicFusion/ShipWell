Sender & Receiver

ONE SHIPMENT.
TWO DASHBOARDS.

Everyone involved follows the same live journey — with the item photos, alerts and next steps that matter to them.

1 · Sender books & uploads photos

Add the parcel details, photos of the item and the receiver's email. ShipWell reviews it before it moves.

2 · Receiver signs up

The moment they register with that same email, the parcel is linked to their account automatically.

3 · “On Its Way To You”

It appears on their dashboard with the sender's photos, live checkpoints and any action we need from them.
